    About

    Scores come from independent benchmarks, with sources. Where there is no source, there is no score.

    How we choose

    We list agents that are widely used, actively developed and clearly useful for a specific job. Facts and prices are checked by hand, and each listing is re-checked about once a month.

    How the coding score works

    Method v1. The score is the share of Terminal-Bench 4.0 tasks the agent solved in its best published run, with the model it used. Terminal-Bench is an independent benchmark of agents doing real work in a terminal. Agents without a published result are listed without a score, after those with one.

    Other categories

    Public benchmarks for support, research, admin and sales mostly test the underlying models, not the products you buy. Until we can measure the products themselves, these categories are listed without scores and in no particular order.

    Independence
